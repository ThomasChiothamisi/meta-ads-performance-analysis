# meta-ads-performance-analysis
This project involved analyzing Meta Ads campaign performance using SQL to uncover insights into engagement, conversions, audience reach, ad effectiveness, and campaign efficiency across Facebook and Instagram. The project simulated a real marketing analytics environment by transforming raw advertising data into actionable business insights. Data was extracted and cleaned through Excel and analyzed using SQL Server, then visualized in Power BI through an interactive dashboard.

**SQL Analysis**

The SQL analysis uses SQL Server to examine campaign performance, user engagement, conversions and purchasing behaviour across the dataset.

Key areas include:

- Click Through Rate
- Conversion Rate
- Performance by Country
- Performance by Target Gender
- Total Clicks
- Total Enagements
- Total Impressions

**Power BI Analysis**

The Power BI analysis includes a data model showing the relationships between the datasets, along with dashboard visualisations presenting key findings and performance trends from the analysis. This dashboard provides a high-level overview of a Meta Ads campaign’s performance, tracking key metrics such as impressions, engagement rates, and conversion rates across different audience segments and ad platforms. It highlights how users interacted with the ads and helps identify which channels and demographics delivered the strongest results. The visual breakdown enables quick performance comparisons and supports data-driven decisions for campaign optimization and budget allocation.

**Key Insights**

- The United States is the strongest overall market on Instagram, leading in both total purchases and conversion rate, indicating strong performance in both scale and efficiency.
- While the United States drives the highest purchase volume across campaigns, Japan and Mexico outperform in conversion efficiency, showing stronger purchase intent per click despite lower overall volume.
- Female and male audiences show very similar engagement rates (Female: 13.88%, Male: 13.44%), but females generate significantly higher engagement volume (13,000 vs 6,000), suggesting greater reach or audience size impact.
- Country-level engagement efficiency varies significantly, with some markets converting traffic far more effectively than others, highlighting uneven audience quality across regions.

**Recommendations**

- Increase budget allocation toward the United States on Instagram, as it delivers both the highest conversion rate and strongest purchase volume.
- Increase budget in Japan and Mexico to evaluate scalability, as these markets show higher conversion efficiency and may provide lower-cost acquisition opportunities.
- Optimize targeting strategy in lower-converting regions by refining audience segmentation and ad creatives to improve click-to-purchase performance.
- Prioritize female audience segment due to significantly higher engagement volume while maintaining similar efficiency levels with male audience.
